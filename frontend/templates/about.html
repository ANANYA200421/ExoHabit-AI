🌌 Interesting Facts About Exoplanets

Turning predictions into stories about distant worlds.

More Planets Than Stars

Scientists estimate that billions of exoplanets exist in the Milky Way galaxy alone. That means there may be more planets than stars – and countless worlds still waiting to be discovered.

The Goldilocks Zone

A planet must orbit at just the right distance from its star for liquid water to exist on the surface – not too hot, not too cold. This orbital sweet spot is called the “Goldilocks zone.”

Extreme Worlds

Some exoplanets are hotter than lava, with temperatures above 2000 °C, while others may hide vast underground oceans. Habitability can appear in surprising and extreme environments.

AI in Space Research

Machine Learning models help astronomers classify and rank potentially habitable planets across massive datasets, revealing patterns that would be impossible to spot by eye alone.

Stellar Influence

A planet’s host star type, radiation, and stability strongly affect its habitability. Two planets with similar orbits can have very different climates if their stars are not alike.

Habitability Score

ExoHabitAI predicts a habitability score, turning complex physical parameters into a single ranking that helps prioritize which exoplanets should be studied next.
